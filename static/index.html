Research controls: model and assumptions

Through-zero assumes the white star provides the geometric zero. A free intercept is an empirical fit; it does not prove a physical grating model. Neither is an independently validated wavelength solution. Fits are unweighted because centroid uncertainties have not been supplied.

What is needed before submitting?

Start by registering your observer code, observing site and spectroscopy equipment with AAVSO, then submit an approved standard star for their validation.

The submission needs a reduced one-dimensional FITS spectrum with a wavelength scale and the required observation headers. Site and equipment names must match your AAVSO records. The app currently exports CSV and evidence files, not an AVSpec submission FITS file.

Our submission checks must follow the current technical guide. A local check cannot guarantee acceptance by AAVSO’s validators.
